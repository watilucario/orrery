Durée : 5 min
Introduction : Every transaction a business makes is recorded in the journal as an entry. An entry affects at least two accounts and is always balanced. This is the double-entry rule. This demo follows the French chart of accounts (plan comptable général), so account numbers are French ones.

## Debit and credit
### Two sides of an account
::: definition Debit and credit
An account has two columns: the **debit** on the left, the **credit** on the right. These are positions, not judgements: a debit is not a loss.
:::

Each account has a number whose first digit gives its class.

| Class | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Capital and long-term funds (share capital, loans) |
| 2 | Fixed assets |
| 3 | Inventory |
| 4 | Third parties (customers, suppliers) |
| 5 | Financial accounts (bank, cash) |
| 6 | Expenses |
| 7 | Revenue |

### Which way does an account increase?
An asset or expense account increases on the debit side. A liability, equity or revenue account increases on the credit side. The opposite side decreases it.

## The journal entry
::: formule
Total debits = Total credits
:::

To record an entry: identify the transaction, find the accounts affected, choose the side of each one, then check the balance. Write the debits first, then the credits.

::: ecriture 12 March · Sale on credit to a customer
D | 411 | Customers | 4 800
C | 707 | Sales of goods | 4 800
Note : the customer owes 4 800 € (VAT ignored); the bank does not move.
:::

::: exemple Purchase of goods on credit
Purchase of 3 000 € from a supplier, paid later: you debit 607 Purchases of goods and credit 401 Suppliers, for 3 000 € on each side.
:::

### When the customer pays
The payment of an invoice is a new entry: you debit 512 Bank and credit 411 Customers. The amount owed disappears, the money arrives.

::: attention Debit does not mean withdrawal
Debiting 512 Bank makes the bank balance **go up**: an asset account increases on the debit side. The word has nothing to do with the bank in your everyday life.
:::

## À retenir
- An entry affects at least two accounts.
- Total debits = total credits, always.
- Debit on the left, credit on the right.
- Assets and expenses increase on the debit side.
- Liabilities, equity and revenue increase on the credit side.
- Classes: 1 capital, 2 fixed assets, 3 inventory, 4 third parties, 5 financial, 6 expenses, 7 revenue.
- Sale on credit: debit 411, credit 707.
- Customer payment: debit 512, credit 411.

Sources : principles of the French general chart of accounts (plan comptable général), simplified; made-up figures.
