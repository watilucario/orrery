Introduction : The result tells you whether the business made or lost money over a period. You calculate it by comparing what it earned (revenue) with what it spent (expenses). It then appears on the liabilities and equity side of the balance sheet.

## Expenses and revenue
### Two families of accounts
::: definition Expense
A cost used up during the period: purchases, pay, rent, interest. Expenses are the **class 6** accounts.
:::

::: definition Revenue
Money earned during the period: sales of goods, services sold. Revenue accounts are the **class 7** accounts.
:::

| Account | Nature |
|---|---|
| 607 Purchases of goods | Expense |
| 613 Rent | Expense |
| 641 Staff pay | Expense |
| 661 Interest expense | Expense |
| 706 Services sold | Revenue |
| 707 Sales of goods | Revenue |

## Calculating the result
::: formule
Result = Revenue − Expenses
:::

A positive result is a **profit**, a negative result a **loss**. Here, income tax is ignored to keep things simple.

::: cascade Alba shop, from sales to result
+ Sales of goods (707) | 90 000
− Purchases of goods (607) | 52 000
= Sales minus purchases | 38 000
− Staff pay (641) | 18 000
− Rent (613) | 7 000
− Interest expense (661) | 1 000
= Profit for the year | 12 000
Unité : €
Note : income tax ignored.
:::

::: exemple The Alba shop's totals
- Revenue: 90 000 €.
- Expenses: 52 000 + 18 000 + 7 000 + 1 000 = 78 000 €.
- Result: 90 000 − 78 000 = 12 000 €, a profit.
:::

## Result and balance sheet
The result for the year is recorded on the liabilities and equity side, in equity: account 120 for a profit, account 129 for a loss. This is what keeps the balance sheet balanced.

::: attention A long-lasting purchase is not an expense
An item used for several years, such as equipment, is a fixed asset (class 2): it stays among the assets. It does not reduce the result in the year it is bought.
:::

## À retenir
- Expenses = class 6, revenue = class 7.
- Result = revenue − expenses.
- Positive: profit. Negative: loss.
- Loan interest is an expense (661).
- A long-lasting item is a fixed asset, not an expense.
- The result is on the liabilities and equity side: account 120 (profit) or 129 (loss).
