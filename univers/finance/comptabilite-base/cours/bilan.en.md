Introduction : The balance sheet is a snapshot of the business on a given date. It shows what it owns and how that was financed. All the rest of accounting exists to build it and keep it right. This demo follows the French chart of accounts (plan comptable général), so account numbers are French ones.

## What the balance sheet shows
### A snapshot on a date
::: definition Balance sheet
A two-column statement that shows **what the business owns** (the assets) and **what it owes or has received** (the liabilities and equity), on a specific date.
:::

On the left, the **assets**: the uses of funds, that is, what the business owns or is owed. On the right, the **liabilities and equity**: the sources of funds, that is, where the money came from (owners' contributions, debts).

### Which accounts go where?
| Side | Example accounts |
|---|---|
| Assets | 218 equipment, 370 inventory, 411 customers, 512 bank |
| Liabilities and equity | 101 share capital, 120 profit, 164 loan, 401 suppliers |

## The balance of the balance sheet
::: formule
Assets = Liabilities and equity
:::

What the business owns has always been financed, by contributions or by debts. The two totals are therefore equal, to the euro. The result for the year is also on the liabilities and equity side: a profit is added, a loss is subtracted.

::: bilan Alba shop on 31 December
Colonnes : Assets | Liabilities and equity
Unité : €
Equipment (218) | 18 000 | cycle long
Inventory (370) | 9 000 | exploitation
Customers (411) | 6 000 | exploitation
Bank (512) | 12 000 | trésorerie
--
Share capital (101) | 20 000 | cycle long
Profit (120) | 12 000 | cycle long
Loan (164) | 8 000 | cycle long
Suppliers (401) | 5 000 | exploitation
Note : 45 000 € on each side.
:::

::: exemple The Alba shop's totals
- Assets: 18 000 + 9 000 + 6 000 + 12 000 = 45 000 €.
- Liabilities and equity: 20 000 + 12 000 + 8 000 + 5 000 = 45 000 €.
:::

::: attention The result is not an omission
If you add up the liabilities and equity without the result, you get 33 000 € and the balance sheet looks wrong. The result for the year is part of the liabilities and equity side.
:::

## À retenir
- The balance sheet is a snapshot of the business's wealth on a date.
- Assets show what the business owns or is owed.
- Liabilities and equity show where the money came from: contributions and debts.
- Assets = liabilities and equity, always.
- Bank, customers, inventory and equipment are assets.
- Share capital, loan, suppliers and the result are on the liabilities and equity side.
- A profit is added to liabilities and equity, a loss is subtracted.
