# Profit or loss

## re1
In accounting, what is an expense?

- [ ] A long-lasting asset the business buys
- [ ] An amount the business still has to pay
- [x] A cost that reduces the profit
- [ ] A cash contribution from the owners

Explication : expenses (class 6) are the costs used up during the period: purchases, pay, rent, interest. A long-lasting asset is a fixed asset, a debt is a liability, and a contribution is share capital.

## re2
The Alba shop has 90 000 € of revenue and 78 000 € of expenses. What is its result?

- [ ] A loss of 12 000 €
- [ ] A profit of 168 000 €
- [ ] A profit of 78 000 €
- [x] A profit of 12 000 €

Explication : result = revenue − expenses = 90 000 − 78 000 = 12 000 €. The sign is positive: it is a profit.

## re3
True or false: buying an office computer for 18 000 € that will last several years is an expense of 18 000 € for the year.

- [x] False
- [ ] True

Ordre fixe : oui

Explication : a long-lasting asset is a fixed asset, class 2: it stays among the assets. Only the part that wears out each year becomes an expense, through depreciation, which this course does not cover.

## re4
Léa works out the Alba shop's result. Sales: 90 000 €. Purchases: 52 000 €. Pay: 18 000 €. Rent: 7 000 €. Loan interest: 1 000 €.

> Step 1: sales, a revenue item, are 90 000 €.
> Step 2: take away the purchases: 90 000 − 52 000 = 38 000 €.
> Step 3: take away pay and rent: 38 000 − 18 000 − 7 000 = 13 000 €.
> Step 4: add the interest: 13 000 + 1 000 = 14 000 €, the result.

Which step contains the mistake?

- [ ] Step 1
- [ ] Step 2
- [ ] Step 3
- [x] Step 4

Niveau : expliquer

Explication : loan interest is an expense (account 661): you subtract it, you do not add it. The result is 13 000 − 1 000 = 12 000 €.

## re5
Put the steps for calculating a result in order.

Type : ordonner

1. Add up the class 7 revenue
2. Add up the class 6 expenses
3. Subtract the expenses from the revenue
4. Read the sign: profit or loss

Explication : you total the two classes, then take the difference revenue − expenses. A positive number is a profit, a negative number a loss.

## re7
Revenue is 50 000 € and expenses are 56 000 €. The business has a ___ of 6 000 €.

Type : trous

- [x] loss

Explication : 50 000 − 56 000 = − 6 000 €. A negative result is a loss; it reduces equity, on the liabilities and equity side.
