# Journal entries

## ec1
In an account, what does the word "debit" mean?

- [ ] A debt owed to a supplier
- [ ] A cash withdrawal from the bank
- [x] The left-hand column of the account
- [ ] A loss made by the business

Explication : debit and credit are the two sides of an account: debit is on the left, credit on the right. They are not everyday words for a debt or a loss.

## ec2
When is a journal entry balanced?

- [x] When total debits equal total credits
- [ ] When it affects exactly two accounts
- [ ] When all its accounts are in the same class
- [ ] When its amount is a round number

Explication : this is the double-entry rule: every entry has as much on the debit side as on the credit side. It can affect more than two accounts and mix classes.

## ec3
You sell 4 800 € of goods to a customer who will pay later (VAT ignored). What is the journal entry?

- [ ] Debit 707 Sales, credit 411 Customers
- [ ] Debit 512 Bank, credit 707 Sales
- [x] Debit 411 Customers, credit 707 Sales
- [ ] Debit 411 Customers, credit 512 Bank

Explication : the customer owes us 4 800 €: you debit 411 Customers (an asset goes up). The sale is revenue: you credit 707 Sales of goods. Nothing comes into the bank until the customer pays.

## ec4
You buy 3 000 € of goods, to be paid to the supplier later (VAT ignored). What is the journal entry?

- [ ] Debit 401 Suppliers, credit 607 Purchases
- [ ] Debit 607 Purchases, credit 512 Bank
- [ ] Debit 512 Bank, credit 401 Suppliers
- [x] Debit 607 Purchases, credit 401 Suppliers

Explication : the purchase is an expense: you debit 607 Purchases of goods. The debt to the supplier goes up: you credit 401 Suppliers. The bank is not affected until payment.

## ec5
Match each account to its class in the chart of accounts.

Type : associer

- 101 Share capital = Class 1
- 411 Customers = Class 4
- 512 Bank = Class 5
- 607 Purchases of goods = Class 6
- 707 Sales of goods = Class 7

Explication : the first digit of an account gives its class: 1 capital and long-term funds, 2 fixed assets, 3 inventory, 4 third parties (customers, suppliers), 5 financial accounts, 6 expenses, 7 revenue.

## ec6
Put the steps for recording a journal entry in order.

Type : ordonner

1. Identify the transaction and its amount
2. Find the accounts affected
3. Choose debit or credit for each account
4. Check that debits and credits are equal

Explication : you start from the transaction, look for the accounts, decide the side of each one, then check the balance before writing the entry in the journal.

## ec7
When the customer pays the invoice, you debit 512 Bank and credit account number ___.

Type : trous

- [x] 411

Explication : the payment clears what the customer owed: you credit 411 Customers. At the same time, the money arrives in the bank: you debit 512 Bank.
