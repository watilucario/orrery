# The balance sheet

## bi1
What does a company's balance sheet show?

- [ ] What it sold and spent during the year
- [x] What it owns and what it owes, on a given date
- [ ] The entries posted day by day in the journal
- [ ] The profit or loss of the past period

Explication : the balance sheet is a snapshot of the company's wealth on a specific date, for example 31 December. Sales and spending over the year belong to the income statement.

## bi2
A business lost money this year. True or false: its assets can then differ from its liabilities and equity.

- [ ] True
- [x] False

Ordre fixe : oui

Explication : assets and liabilities plus equity are always equal. A loss does not break the equality: it reduces equity, on the liabilities and equity side.

## bi3
Which of these accounts is an asset on the balance sheet?

- [ ] 101 Share capital
- [ ] 164 Loans from credit institutions
- [ ] 401 Suppliers
- [x] 512 Bank

Explication : the bank is cash on hand, so it is something the company uses: it goes with the assets. Share capital, loans and suppliers are sources of funds: they go on the liabilities and equity side.

## bi4
Match each account to its nature.

Type : associer

- 411 Customers = Asset: what customers owe us
- 401 Suppliers = Liability: what we owe our suppliers
- 101 Share capital = Equity: what the owners put in
- 512 Bank = Asset: the cash available in the bank
- 218 Other tangible fixed assets = Asset: a long-lasting item we use

Explication : assets are what the company owns or is owed (customers, bank, long-lasting items). Liabilities and equity are what it owes or what has been put in (suppliers, share capital).

## bi5
Put the steps for checking a balance sheet in order.

Type : ordonner

1. Sort each account into assets or liabilities and equity
2. Add up all the asset accounts
3. Add up all the liability and equity accounts
4. Compare the two totals: they must be equal

Explication : you sort the accounts first, then add up each side, and finish with the comparison. Two different totals point to an omission or a mistake.

## bi6
On a balance sheet, total assets always equal total ___.

Type : trous

- [x] liabilities

Explication : what the company owns (assets) was financed by contributions and debts (liabilities and equity). The two totals are therefore equal.

## bi7
The Alba shop's balance sheet contains: equipment 18 000 €, inventory 9 000 €, customers 6 000 €, bank 12 000 €, share capital 20 000 €, profit for the year 12 000 €, loan 8 000 €, suppliers 5 000 €. Léa builds the balance sheet.

> Step 1: the assets are the equipment, inventory, customers and bank.
> Step 2: total assets are 18 000 + 9 000 + 6 000 + 12 000 = 45 000 €.
> Step 3: the liabilities and equity are the share capital, loan and suppliers, which makes 33 000 €.
> Step 4: the totals are equal: 45 000 € in assets, 33 000 € in liabilities and equity.

Which step contains the mistake?

- [ ] Step 1
- [ ] Step 2
- [x] Step 3
- [ ] Step 4

Niveau : expliquer

Explication : step 3 leaves out the profit for the year (12 000 €), which is on the liabilities and equity side: 20 000 + 12 000 + 8 000 + 5 000 = 45 000 €. The totals in step 4 are therefore wrong too, but the mistake starts in step 3.

## bi8
The Alba shop makes a profit of 12 000 €. Where do you find it on the balance sheet?

- [ ] On the assets side, in 512 Bank
- [x] In equity, in 120 Profit for the year
- [ ] On the assets side, in 411 Customers
- [ ] In debts, in 401 Suppliers

Explication : the profit belongs to the owners: it is added to equity, on the liabilities and equity side, in account 120 Profit for the year (profit). The bank balance may have gone up, but that is a different matter.
