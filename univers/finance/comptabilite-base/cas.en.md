## alba · Alba shop closes its books
Thème : bilan

> The Alba shop is closing its financial year. Here are its balances on 31 December (VAT and income tax ignored). Account numbers follow the French chart of accounts.
>
> | Account | Balance |
> |---|---|
> | 218 Other tangible fixed assets | 18 000 € |
> | 370 Merchandise inventory | 9 000 € |
> | 411 Customers | 6 000 € |
> | 512 Bank | 12 000 € |
> | 101 Share capital | 20 000 € |
> | 164 Loans | 8 000 € |
> | 401 Suppliers | 5 000 € |
> | 707 Sales of goods | 90 000 € |
> | 607 Purchases of goods | 52 000 € |
> | 641 Staff pay | 18 000 € |
> | 613 Rent | 7 000 € |
> | 661 Interest expense | 1 000 € |

### alba1
What is the total of the assets?

- [ ] 33 000 €
- [x] 45 000 €
- [ ] 51 000 €
- [ ] 78 000 €

Explication : the assets are accounts 218, 370, 411 and 512: 18 000 + 9 000 + 6 000 + 12 000 = 45 000 €. Accounts in classes 6 and 7 are not on the balance sheet.

### alba2
What is the profit or loss for the year?

- [ ] A loss of 12 000 €
- [ ] A profit of 38 000 €
- [x] A profit of 12 000 €
- [ ] A profit of 90 000 €

Explication : revenue = 90 000 €. Expenses = 52 000 + 18 000 + 7 000 + 1 000 = 78 000 €. Result = 90 000 − 78 000 = 12 000 €, a profit.

### alba3
What is the total of liabilities and equity, including the profit for the year?

- [x] 45 000 €
- [ ] 33 000 €
- [ ] 57 000 €
- [ ] 90 000 €

Explication : share capital 20 000 + profit 12 000 + loans 8 000 + suppliers 5 000 = 45 000 €. Without the profit, you would get 33 000 €. The total equals the total of the assets.

### alba4
A customer pays 2 000 € of what they owe by bank transfer. What happens to the total of the assets?

- [ ] It falls to 43 000 €
- [ ] It rises to 47 000 €
- [ ] It falls to 33 000 €
- [x] It stays at 45 000 €

Explication : you debit 512 Bank (12 000 + 2 000 = 14 000 €) and credit 411 Customers (6 000 − 2 000 = 4 000 €). One asset line goes up and the other goes down by the same amount, so the total does not change.
