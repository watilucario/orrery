# Exercises

## e1 · Alba shop's profit or loss
Thème : resultat
Durée conseillée : 10 min
Source : Course sheet · resultat

> The Alba shop recorded these accounts over the year (VAT and income tax ignored).
>
> | Account | Amount |
> |---|---|
> | 707 Sales of goods | 90 000 € |
> | 607 Purchases of goods | 52 000 € |
> | 641 Staff pay | 18 000 € |
> | 613 Rent | 7 000 € |
> | 661 Interest expense | 1 000 € |

### Question 1
What is the total of the expenses?

Réponse : 78 000 €
Calcul : 52 000 + 18 000 + 7 000 + 1 000 = 78 000.
Indice : expenses are the class 6 accounts; do not include the sales.

### Question 2
What is the profit or loss for the year?

Réponse : 12 000 €
Calcul : revenue − expenses = 90 000 − 78 000 = 12 000.
Indice : there is only one revenue account here, in class 7.

### Question 3
What share of sales does the result represent, as a percentage rounded to two decimals?

Réponse : 13,33 %
Tolérance : 0,05
Calcul : 12 000 ÷ 90 000 × 100 = 13,33 %.
Indice : divide the result by the sales, then multiply by 100.

### Question 4
Where do you find the result on the balance sheet, and why?

Attendu : On the liabilities and equity side, in equity (account 120 for a profit). The profit belongs to the owners, and it keeps the balance sheet balanced: without it, the assets would exceed the liabilities and equity.
