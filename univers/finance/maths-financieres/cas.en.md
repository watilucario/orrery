# Cas

## offres · Two savings offers
Thème : composes

> You have €10,000 to invest, and you won't touch it. Two offers:
> | Offer | Annual rate | Interest |
> |---|---|---|
> | A | 4 % | simple |
> | B | 3.5 % | compound |

### offres1
How much interest does offer A earn after 5 years?

- [ ] €400
- [x] €2,000
- [ ] €2,200
- [ ] €12,000

Explication : I = 10,000 × 0.04 × 5 = €2,000. €12,000 is the accumulated value, which includes the principal.

Source : Course sheet · simple interest

### offres2
What is the accumulated value of offer B after 5 years?

- [ ] €11,750.00
- [ ] €11,800.00
- [x] €11,876.86
- [ ] €12,000.00

Explication : 10,000 × 1.035⁵ = 10,000 × 1.187686 ≈ €11,876.86. €11,750 would be the value with simple interest at 3.5 %.

Source : Course sheet · compound interest

### offres3
After 5 years, which offer earns more, and by how much?

- [x] A, by €123.14
- [ ] B, by €123.14
- [ ] A, by €2,000
- [ ] They earn the same amount

Explication : A gives €12,000 and B gives €11,876.86, a gap of €123.14 in favour of A. Over this period, A's higher rate still wins.

Source : Course sheet · compound interest

### offres4
Same question after 10 years: which offer comes out ahead?

- [ ] A, with €14,000 against €13,500
- [x] B, with €14,105.99 against €14,000
- [ ] Neither: both give €14,000
- [ ] B, with €13,500 against €14,000

Explication : A gives 10,000 × (1 + 0.04 × 10) = €14,000. B gives 10,000 × 1.035¹⁰ ≈ €14,105.99. Over time, compound interest overtakes simple interest despite a lower rate.

Source : Course sheet · compound interest
