# Exercices

## x1 · A six-year investment
Thème : composes
Niveau : faire
Durée conseillée : 8 min
Source : Course sheet · compound interest. Made-up figures, solution worked out with the course.

> You invest €8,000 at 3 % a year for 6 years.
> Interest is compounded every year and you withdraw nothing.
> For comparison, we also calculate the accumulated value with simple interest at the same rate.

### Question 1
What is the accumulated value after 6 years with compound interest?

Réponse : 9 552,42 €
Tolérance : 0,05
Calcul : 8,000 × 1.03⁶ = 8,000 × 1.194052 ≈ €9,552.42.
Indice : multiply the principal by the factor 1 + i raised to the power 6.

### Question 2
What is the difference from the accumulated value with simple interest?

Réponse : 112,42 €
Tolérance : 0,05
Calcul : with simple interest, 8,000 × (1 + 0.03 × 6) = 8,000 × 1.18 = €9,440. Difference: 9,552.42 − 9,440 = €112.42.
Indice : first work out the accumulated value with simple interest using C × (1 + i × n).

### Question 3
Explain why compound interest earns more than simple interest, and what happens to the difference if the term goes up to 12 years.

Attendu : with compound interest, each year's interest is added to the principal and earns interest in turn, whereas with simple interest it is only calculated on the starting principal. The gap widens with time: at 12 years it is much larger than at 6 years, because (1 + i)ⁿ moves further and further away from 1 + i × n.
