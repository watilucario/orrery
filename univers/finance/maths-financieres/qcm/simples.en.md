# Simple interest

## s1
You invest €1,000 at 5 % a year with simple interest for 3 years. What is the total interest?

- [ ] €50
- [x] €150
- [ ] €157.63
- [ ] €1,150

Explication : simple interest = principal × rate × time = 1,000 × 0.05 × 3 = €150. €1,150 is the accumulated value, not the interest.

Source : Definition of simple interest: I = C × i × n

## s2
You invest €2,000 at 4 % a year with simple interest for 2 years. What is the accumulated value?

- [ ] €160
- [ ] €2,080
- [x] €2,160
- [ ] €2,163.20

Explication : interest = 2,000 × 0.04 × 2 = €160. Accumulated value = 2,000 + 160 = €2,160. €2,163.20 would be the value with compound interest.

Source : Definition of simple interest: I = C × i × n

## s3
A principal of €800 earns €48 of simple interest in 1 year. What is the annual rate?

- [ ] 4 %
- [ ] 5 %
- [x] 6 %
- [ ] 8 %

Explication : i = I / (C × n) = 48 / (800 × 1) = 0.06, or 6 %.

Source : Definition of simple interest: I = C × i × n

## s4
With simple interest, when the term doubles (same principal, same rate), the interest…

- [ ] stays the same
- [x] is multiplied by 2
- [ ] is multiplied by 4
- [ ] rises by less than 2 times

Explication : I = C × i × n is proportional to n: doubling n doubles I.

Source : Definition of simple interest: I = C × i × n

## s5
True or false: with simple interest, one year's interest is added to the principal and itself earns interest the following year.

- [ ] True
- [x] False

Ordre fixe : oui

Explication : that is the principle of compound interest. With simple interest, interest is calculated each year on the starting principal only, so it stays the same from one year to the next.

## s6
You invest €1,000 at 3 % a year with simple interest for 4 years. The total interest is € ___.
Type : trous

- [x] 120

Explication : I = C × i × n = 1,000 × 0.03 × 4 = €120. Each year earns €30 and 4 × 30 = €120.

## s7
> Principal: €1,500
> Rate: 4 % a year, simple interest
> Term: 3 years

A student calculates the accumulated value. Which step of their reasoning is wrong?

- [ ] They write I = C × i × n.
- [ ] They calculate I = 1,500 × 0.04 × 3 = €180.
- [x] They subtract the interest: 1,500 − 180 = €1,320.
- [ ] They check it against €60 of interest per year.

Niveau : expliquer

Explication : the accumulated value is the principal plus the interest: 1,500 + 180 = €1,680. The student subtracted instead of adding. The €60 per year (1,500 × 0.04) is correct.
