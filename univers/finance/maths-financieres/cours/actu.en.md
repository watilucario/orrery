Introduction : €100 today is worth more than €100 in a year, because you can invest it. Discounting brings a future sum back to its value today. It is used to compare cash flows that do not happen on the same date.

## Why discount
### A sum has a different value depending on its date
A sum received later is worth less than a sum received now, because the second can already earn interest. To compare two amounts, we bring them to the same date: today.

::: definition Discounting
An operation that calculates today's value of a sum to be received or paid in n years. It is the reverse of compounding.
:::

## Calculating the present value
### Divide instead of multiplying
::: formule
C₀ = Cₙ / (1 + i)ⁿ
:::

- **Cₙ**: future sum, in euros.
- **i**: annual discount rate, as a decimal.
- **n**: number of years before you receive it.

::: exemple €1,210 in 2 years, rate of 10 %
Present value = 1,210 / 1.10² = 1,210 / 1.21 = €1,000.
:::

::: etapes Calculating a present value
Identify | the future sum, the rate and the term
Calculate | the factor (1 + i)ⁿ
Divide | the future sum by this factor
Conclude | today's value, always lower
:::

::: attention Don't multiply
Multiplying by (1 + i)ⁿ gives an accumulated value, not a present value. The present value is always lower than the future sum when the rate is positive.
:::

## What makes the present value change
### The rate and the term
When the rate goes up, the divisor (1 + i)ⁿ goes up and the present value goes down. The same happens when the sum is further away.

::: barres €1,000 received later, rate of 10 %
In 1 year | 909,09
In 2 years | 826,45
In 3 years | 751,31
In 5 years | 620,92
Unité : €
Note : the further away the date, the lower today's value.
:::

## À retenir
- Discounting means bringing a future sum back to today.
- C₀ = Cₙ / (1 + i)ⁿ.
- Discounting is the reverse of compounding.
- Positive rate: the present value is lower than the future sum.
- The higher the rate, the lower the present value.
- The further away the sum, the lower the present value.
- You compare sums by bringing them to the same date.
