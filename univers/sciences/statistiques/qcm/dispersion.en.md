# Spread: range, standard deviation, quartiles

## d1
Eight scores out of 20: 8; 10; 10; 12; 13; 14; 15; 18. What is the range?

- [ ] 8
- [x] 10
- [ ] 12.5
- [ ] 18

Explication : range = largest value − smallest value = 18 − 8 = 10 points. The 12.5 is the mean of the series.

## d2
Series: 3; 5; 5; 7 (its mean is 5). What is its population variance, that is, dividing by n?

- [ ] 1.41
- [x] 2
- [ ] 4
- [ ] 8

Explication : the gaps to the mean are −2, 0, 0 and 2. Their squares are 4, 0, 0 and 4, which makes 8 in total. Variance = 8 / 4 = 2. The 1.41 is the standard deviation (√2), and 8 is the sum of squares.

## d3
True or false: if you add 5 points to every score in a class, the standard deviation goes up by 5.

- [x] False
- [ ] True

Ordre fixe : oui

Explication : all the gaps to the mean stay the same, since the mean also rises by 5. The mean changes, the standard deviation does not move.

## d5
Put the steps for computing a standard deviation in order.
Type : ordonner

1. Compute the mean of the series.
2. Compute the gap of each value to the mean.
3. Square each gap.
4. Divide the sum of squares by n (or n − 1): this is the variance.
5. Take the square root of the variance.

Explication : first you centre on the mean, square the gaps so they do not cancel out, average them, then the square root brings the result back to the units of the data.

## d6
Match each measure with what it describes.
Type : associer

- Range = The gap between the smallest and the largest value
- Variance = The average of the squared gaps to the mean
- Standard deviation = The typical distance from the mean, in the data's unit
- Interquartile range = The width of the zone holding the central half of the values

Explication : the range looks only at the two extremes; the variance and standard deviation look at all values; the interquartile range (Q3 − Q1) ignores the lowest quarter and the highest quarter.

## d7
Seven salaries: 1,700; 1,800; 1,800; 1,900; 2,000; 2,100; 2,300 €. Take the median of each half (the median 1,900 is set aside). What is the interquartile range?

- [ ] €100
- [ ] €400
- [x] €300
- [ ] €600

Explication : the lower half (1,700; 1,800; 1,800) has median Q1 = 1,800. The upper half (2,000; 2,100; 2,300) has median Q3 = 2,100. Q3 − Q1 = €300. The 600 is the range.

## d8
Same series 3; 5; 5; 7, but seen as a sample (divide by n − 1). What is the variance, rounded to the nearest hundredth?

- [ ] 2.00
- [ ] 4.00
- [ ] 8.00
- [x] 2.67

Explication : the sum of squared gaps is 8. Sample variance = 8 / (4 − 1) = 8 / 3 ≈ 2.67. With n instead of n − 1, you would get 2.00 (population variance).
