# Reading a statistic

## l1
In a newspaper, the vertical axis of a chart starts at 1,180. Two bars are worth 1,200 and 1,230, giving visible heights of 20 and 50. By how much does the 2nd value really exceed the 1st?

- [ ] 30%
- [ ] 150%
- [x] 2.5%
- [ ] 25%

Explication : 1,230 / 1,200 = 1.025, which is +2.5%. On screen the bar looks 2.5 times taller (+150%) because the axis is truncated. The 30 is a gap in value, not in percent.

## l2
The unemployment rate goes from 8% to 10%. Which sentence is correct?

- [ ] It rises by 2%.
- [ ] It rises by 20%.
- [x] It rises by 2 points, or +25%.
- [ ] It rises by 10 points.

Explication : 10 − 8 = 2 percentage points. In relative terms, 2 / 8 = 0.25: +25% compared with the starting level. Saying "+2%" mixes the two.

## l3
True or false: if two series of numbers move together, one must be the cause of the other.

- [ ] True
- [x] False

Ordre fixe : oui

Explication : a correlation says two quantities vary together, not that one causes the other. A common cause, or chance, can explain it all.

## l4
Every summer, ice cream sales and the number of sunburns rise together. Which explanation is the most reasonable?

- [ ] Eating ice cream causes sunburns.
- [ ] Sunburns make people want ice cream.
- [x] Sunny weather pushes both up at once.
- [ ] Chance alone explains it, with no link at all.

Explication : a third variable, here sunny weather, acts on both series. It is called a common cause (or confounding variable).

## l5
A poll of 1,000 randomly chosen people gives 52% in favour. Using the rough rule 1 / √n, what is the margin of error at 95% confidence?

- [ ] ±0.3 points
- [x] ±3 points
- [ ] ±10 points
- [ ] ±30 points

Explication : 1 / √1,000 ≈ 0.032, about 3 points. The true result is probably between 49% and 55%, so you cannot claim that a majority is in favour.

## l6
For the same poll, the larger the sample, the ___ the margin of error.
Type : trous

- [x] smaller

Explication : the margin varies as 1 / √n. It shrinks as n grows, but slowly: to halve it, you need four times as many people.

## l7
Match each trap with the question that helps you spot it.
Type : associer

- Truncated axis = Where does the chart's vertical axis start?
- Points or percent = Is the gap measured against the starting value?
- Correlation = Could another cause explain both series?
- Small sample = How many people answered, and how were they chosen?

Explication : four reading reflexes. Each boils down to one simple question to ask before believing a number or a chart.

## l8
A price rises by 20%, then falls by 20%. Compared with the starting price, it has…

- [ ] returned exactly to its level
- [x] fallen by 4%
- [ ] fallen by 2%
- [ ] risen by 4%

Explication : 100 × 1.20 = 120, then 120 × 0.80 = 96. The final price is 96% of the starting price, i.e. −4%. The fall applies to a larger base than the rise.
