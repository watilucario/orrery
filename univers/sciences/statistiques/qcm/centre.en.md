# Centre: mean, median, mode

## c1
What is the mean of the four scores 12, 15, 9 and 14?

- [ ] 11.5
- [ ] 12
- [x] 12.5
- [ ] 13

Explication : the sum is 12 + 15 + 9 + 14 = 50, and 50 / 4 = 12.5. The 13 is a trap: it is the median (the mean of 12 and 14 once the series is sorted).

## c2
Seven monthly salaries, in euros: 1,700; 1,800; 1,800; 1,900; 2,000; 2,100; 2,300. What is the median?

- [ ] €1,800
- [x] €1,900
- [ ] €1,943
- [ ] €2,000

Explication : the series is already sorted and has 7 values. The median is the 4th: €1,900. The 1,800 is the mode, and 1,943 is the mean (13,600 / 7, rounded to the nearest euro).

## c3
True or false: the median of a series is always one of its values.

- [ ] True
- [x] False

Ordre fixe : oui

Explication : with an even number of values, you take the mean of the two middle values. For 2; 4; 6; 8, the median is (4 + 6) / 2 = 5, which is not in the series.

## c4
> Series: 6; 8; 9; 11; 16

Lea computes the mean of this series. Which step contains the error?

- [ ] There are 5 values, so we will divide the sum by 5.
- [x] The sum is 6 + 8 + 9 + 11 + 16 = 48.
- [ ] So the mean is 48 / 5 = 9.6.
- [ ] 9.6 is still between the smallest and the largest value.

Ordre fixe : oui

Explication : the correct sum is 6 + 8 + 9 + 11 + 16 = 50, so the mean is 50 / 5 = 10. The later steps are logical, but they rest on a wrong sum.

## c5
Match each measure with the question it answers.
Type : associer

- Mean = How much would each person get if the total were shared equally?
- Median = Which value has as many values below it as above it?
- Mode = Which value comes up most often in the series?

Explication : the mean comes from the total, the median from rank, the mode from frequency. Three different summaries of the same series.

## c6
In the salary series 1,700; 1,800; 1,800; 1,900; 2,000; 2,100; 2,300, the last salary (€2,300) is replaced by €9,000. What happens to the median?

- [ ] It moves to €2,000.
- [ ] It moves to €2,900.
- [ ] It moves to €5,450.
- [x] It stays at €1,900.

Explication : the median depends on rank, not on how large the extremes are. The 4th value is still €1,900. The mean, however, goes from €1,943 to €2,900 (20,300 / 7).

## c7
In the series 3; 5; 5; 7; 9, the value that comes up most often is called the ___.
Type : trous

- [x] mode

Explication : the mode is the most frequent value. Here, it is 5 (twice). A series can have several modes, or none if all its values are different.

## c8
In a team, one executive earns ten times more than the others. Which indicator best gives the ordinary salary?

- [ ] The mean, because it uses every value
- [x] The median, because an extreme barely moves it
- [ ] The mode, because it is always unique and stable
- [ ] The sum, because it keeps every amount

Explication : the mean is pulled up by the isolated salary. The median stays in the middle of the ordinary salaries. The mode can be multiple, and the sum summarises nothing.
