## notes · Analysing the scores of a test
Thème : dispersion

> Eight students got these scores out of 20 on a test (made-up data).
> | Student | Score |
> |---|---|
> | A | 8 |
> | B | 10 |
> | C | 10 |
> | D | 12 |
> | E | 13 |
> | F | 14 |
> | G | 15 |
> | H | 18 |
> Sum of the scores: 100.

### n1
What is the class mean?

- [ ] 10
- [ ] 12
- [x] 12.5
- [ ] 13.5

Explication : 100 / 8 = 12.5. The 10 is the mode (two students), and 13.5 is the mean of 13 and 14.

### n2
For each student, you compute the gap to the mean of 12.5, then square it. What is the sum of these eight squares?

- [ ] 0
- [ ] 36
- [ ] 100
- [x] 72

Explication : the gaps are −4.5; −2.5; −2.5; −0.5; 0.5; 1.5; 2.5; 5.5. Their squares: 20.25; 6.25; 6.25; 0.25; 0.25; 2.25; 6.25; 30.25. Total: 72. The sum of the unsquared gaps is 0, which is why we square them.

### n3
These eight students are the whole class. What is the population standard deviation?

- [ ] 2.83
- [x] 3.00
- [ ] 9.00
- [ ] 72.00

Explication : variance = 72 / 8 = 9. Standard deviation = √9 = 3.00 points. The 9 is the variance, not the standard deviation.

### n4
Now treat these eight students as a sample from a larger group. What is the sample standard deviation, rounded to the nearest hundredth?

- [ ] 2.83
- [ ] 3.00
- [x] 3.21
- [ ] 3.46

Explication : variance = 72 / (8 − 1) ≈ 10.29. Standard deviation = √10.29 ≈ 3.21. Dividing by n − 1 gives a slightly larger result than 3.00.
