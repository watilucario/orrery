# Exercices

## e1 · The commute times of seven students
Thème : dispersion
Durée conseillée : 10 min
Source : Exercise made up for the demo · course sheet · centre and dispersion

> Commute time, in minutes, of seven students to their school (made-up data):
> | Student | Commute (min) |
> |---|---|
> | 1 | 12 |
> | 2 | 15 |
> | 3 | 15 |
> | 4 | 18 |
> | 5 | 20 |
> | 6 | 25 |
> | 7 | 35 |
> Sum of the commutes: 140 minutes.

### Question 1
What is the mean commute time?

Réponse : 20 min
Calcul : 140 / 7 = 20.
Indice : divide the sum given in the statement by the number of students.

### Question 2
What is the median commute time?

Réponse : 18 min
Calcul : the series is sorted and has 7 values; the median is the 4th, which is 18.
Indice : find the value with three students on each side.

### Question 3
What is the population standard deviation, rounded to the nearest hundredth? These seven students are the only ones concerned.

Réponse : 7,25 min
Tolérance : 0,02
Calcul : gaps to 20: −8; −5; −5; −2; 0; 5; 15. Squares: 64; 25; 25; 4; 0; 25; 225. Sum: 368. Variance: 368 / 7 ≈ 52.57. Standard deviation: √52.57 ≈ 7.25.
Indice : first compute the sum of the squared gaps, then divide by n and take the square root.

### Question 4
The mean (20 min) is higher than the median (18 min). Explain why, and say which of the two better describes the commute of an ordinary student.

Attendu : The 35-minute commute is a high, isolated value: it pulls the mean up without changing the median, which depends only on rank. The median (18 min) better describes an ordinary student's commute, since 6 students out of 7 travel 25 minutes or less.
