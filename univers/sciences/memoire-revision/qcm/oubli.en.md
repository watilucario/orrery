# The forgetting curve

## o1
What does the forgetting curve describe?

- [ ] How fast you read a new text
- [x] The decline of memory over time without review
- [ ] How many pages you retain in an hour
- [ ] The rise of memory after each night of sleep

Explication : it shows how the share of what you still know declines when you review nothing. It measures neither reading speed nor the amount learned.

## o2
Once you have learned something, you forget it at a constant rate.

- [ ] True
- [x] False

Ordre fixe : oui

Explication : the curve drops fast at first, then more and more slowly. A constant rate would give a straight line, not a curve.

## o3
In 1885, ___ published experiments on his own memory, carried out with nonsense syllables.

Type : trous

- [x] Ebbinghaus

Explication : Hermann Ebbinghaus learned lists of nonsense syllables, then measured how much effort it took to relearn them later.

## o4
Match each term with its definition.

Type : associer

- Forgetting curve = Decline of memory as time passes
- Stability = Number of days after which R falls to 90%
- Probability of recall = Estimated chance you remember right now
- Difficulty = Measure, from 1 to 10, of how hard a card is

Explication : stability and probability of recall (R) are two quantities linked by the formula in the sheet. Difficulty, for its part, describes the card.

## o8
Put in order Ebbinghaus's method for measuring forgetting.

Type : ordonner

1. He learns a list of nonsense syllables
2. He waits for a certain delay
3. He relearns the same list
4. He compares this effort with that of the first learning

Explication : what he saves when relearning measures how much remained from the first time.
