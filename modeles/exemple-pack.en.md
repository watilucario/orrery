# Cours · ordre

Durée : 3 min
Introduction : The solar system is made of the Sun and the eight planets that go around it. Knowing them in order is the starting point for everything else.

## Four rocky planets, four giants
The first four planets are rocky and small: Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars. The next four are giants: Jupiter and Saturn (mostly gas), then Uranus and Neptune (the "ice giants").

::: definition Planet
A body that goes around the Sun, massive enough to be round, and that has cleared the neighborhood around its orbit.
:::

## The landmarks to know
Jupiter is the biggest planet. Venus is the hottest, because of its thick atmosphere, even though Mercury is closer to the Sun. Mars is red because of the iron oxide in its soil.

## À retenir
- Eight planets: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune.
- Four rocky ones, then four giants.
- Jupiter is the biggest, Venus the hottest.

Sources : General astronomy knowledge.

# Cours · mouvements

Durée : 2 min
Introduction : The planets don't stay in place: they go around the Sun and spin on themselves. This is what an orrery shows, a mechanical model of the solar system.

## Two movements
Revolution is one trip of a planet around the Sun: a little over 365 days for the Earth. Rotation is one turn of a planet on itself: about 24 hours for the Earth, which gives day and night.

::: attention A common mistake
The Sun does not go around the Earth. It seems to move because the Earth spins on itself.
:::

## Sunlight
Light takes a little over 8 minutes to come from the Sun to us.

## À retenir
- Revolution: one trip around the Sun (a year for the Earth).
- Rotation: one turn on itself (a day for the Earth).
- Sunlight takes about 8 minutes to reach us.

Sources : General astronomy knowledge.
